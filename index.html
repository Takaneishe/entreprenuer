My Love of Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship has allowed me to combine my passions for culinary arts, fashion design, and web development into one creative journey of lifelong learning.

chef_hat

Designing Is My Passion

The reason why I love designing is that it all started with me being a chef. Being a chef is the ultimate designer because you are putting together dishes from recipes. Once you build yourself a skillset from being a student in culinary arts, or maybe just learning to cook at a neighborhood restaurant or fast-food chain, you have placed yourself in a hospitality design sphere of knowledge.

star

Cooking Mechanics

When I graduated at eighteen years old, I was off to the races. Knowing I was learning a skill that I would have for the rest of my life was always a plus. Knowing I was making people happy when they came into the restaurant to enjoy a meal was rewarding.

Taking proteins, vegetables, sauces, and spices and combining them together allowed me to transform ordinary ingredients into works of art. Every plate became an opportunity to showcase creativity and craftsmanship.

Entrepreneurship Inspiration

Entrepreneurship has taught me to never stop learning and never stop creating. Every chapter of my journey has introduced new opportunities to develop skills, solve problems, and grow personally and professionally.

The skills I learned as a chef became the foundation for my interest in fashion design, branding, and ultimately web development. Each experience contributed to building a stronger entrepreneurial mindset.
